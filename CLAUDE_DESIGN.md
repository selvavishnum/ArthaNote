## BRAND IDENTITY

### App Name
- **Product name:** ArthaNote
- **Tamil meaning:** அர்த்த நோட் — "Artha" (money/meaning in Sanskrit/Tamil) + "Note" (ledger/record)
- **Tagline options:**
  - "உங்கள் கடை, உங்கள் கணக்கு" *(Your shop, your accounts)*
  - "Small shop. Big dreams."
  - "The ledger that never sleeps."
  - "கணக்கு இனி கஷ்டமில்லை" *(Accounting is no longer hard)*

### Brand Colors
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Primary Green | `#065f46` | Headers, CTAs, primary buttons |
| Secondary Green | `#059669` | Accents, badges, success states |
| Light Green | `#d1fae5` | Backgrounds, cards |
| White | `#ffffff` | Card backgrounds, clean space |
| Warm Black | `#111827` | Body text |
| Rupee Gold | `#f59e0b` | Highlights, premium callouts |
| Alert Red | `#dc2626` | Debt/credit owing, warnings |

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, large, emotional — Tamil script first when bilingual
- **Body:** Clean, minimal — never more than 3 lines per slide
- **Numbers:** Always large and prominent (₹ amounts are the hero)
- **Font feel:** Rounded, warm, approachable — NOT corporate

### Logo Usage
- Green background → White logo/text
- White background → Dark green logo/text
- Never stretch, never place on busy backgrounds
- Icon: stylized ledger/book with rupee symbol

## TARGET AUDIENCE

### Primary Persona — "Murugan Anna" (Shop Owner)
- **Age:** 28–55
- **Location:** Tamil Nadu (Tier 2 & 3 cities — Coimbatore, Madurai, Salem, Tirupur, Erode, Dindigul, Vellore, Trichy)
- **Business:** Kirana/grocery, vegetables, tea shop, textile, hardware, medical shop
- **Pain:** Paper notebooks, lost entries, forgotten credit (உதிரி), no reports
- **Tech level:** Uses WhatsApp daily, has a smartphone, NOT a power user
- **Language:** Tamil primary, some English okay
- **Emotion driver:** Dignity, control, respect — wants to feel like a "proper businessman"

### Secondary Persona — "Meena Aunty" (Chit Fund Organizer)
- **Age:** 35–60
- **Location:** Suburban Tamil Nadu, apartment communities
- **Business:** Runs neighborhood chit groups of 10–30 members
- **Pain:** Manual tracking in notebooks, member disputes, missed payments
- **Emotion driver:** Trust, reliability, fairness — wants everyone to see the same numbers

### Secondary Persona — "Priya" (Multi-shop Owner)
- **Age:** 30–45
- **Business:** 2–4 shops, has staff, needs access control
- **Pain:** Can't monitor all shops at once, staff errors, no consolidated reports
- **Emotion driver:** Growth, expansion, ambition — she's scaling up

## EMOTIONAL THEMES (Use these in ALL copy)

### 1. Generational Pride
> "அப்பா notebook-ல எழுதினார். நான் ArthaNote-ல பார்க்கிறேன்."
> *(Appa wrote in a notebook. I see it in ArthaNote.)*

The shop is inherited pride. Technology honors it, doesn't replace it.

### 2. Loss Prevention (Fear → Relief)
> "ஒரு நாள் 500 ரூபாய் உதிரி மறந்தேன். அன்றிலிருந்து ArthaNote."
> *(One day I forgot ₹500 credit. Since then, ArthaNote.)*

Lost money is not just money — it's betrayal by your own memory.

### 3. Family Security
> "இரவு கடை மூடும்போது, என் குடும்பம் பத்திரமா இருக்கா?"
> *(When the shop closes at night, is my family safe?)*

Every rupee tracked = one more brick in the house they're building.

### 4. Respect / Dignity
> "Bank manager போல நம்ம கணக்கு வையுங்க."
> *(Keep your accounts like a bank manager.)*

Small shop owners deserve the same tools as big businesses.

### 5. Time (Freedom from Drudgery)
> "மணிக்கணக்ல போன வேலை இப்போ seconds-ல."
> *(Hours of work now done in seconds.)*

Time saved = time with family, time to grow, time to breathe.

### 6. No Ads = Respect
> "உங்கள் நேரம் விளம்பரத்துக்கு இல்லை. உங்கள் கடைக்கு."
> *(Your time is not for ads. It's for your shop.)*

## INSTAGRAM CONTENT SYSTEM

### Carousel Slide Rules
1. **Slide 1 (Hook):** Tamil line + English translation. ONE emotion. No features yet.
2. **Slide 2 (Pain):** Story of the problem. 3–4 short lines. No solution yet.
3. **Slides 3–8 (Features):** 2–3 features per slide, grouped by theme. Emotional one-liner at bottom.
4. **Slide 9 (Differentiator):** The "No Ads" or "Free" or "Tamil first" angle.
5. **Slide 10 (CTA):** Emotional close + download prompt + link in bio.

### Caption Formula
```
[Tamil emotional opening line] 🌿

[English translation in italics]

✅ [Feature 1]
✅ [Feature 2]
✅ [Feature 3]
[... up to 6]

[1-line emotional close sentence]

📲 Download free — link in bio.

[25–30 hashtags]
```

## CONTENT TONE RULES

### Always
- Lead with emotion, follow with feature
- Use Tamil first, English second (except for all-English posts)
- Mention specific Tamil Nadu cities when geo-targeting
- Keep sentences short — 8 words max per line
- Use ₹ symbol, not "Rs" or "INR"
- Reference real shop types: kirana, vegetables, tea shop, textile

### Never
- Use corporate jargon ("synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem")
- Make it sound like a bank or government app
- Use stock photos of foreign-looking people
- Show competitor apps or mention competitor names
- Overclaim ("best app in India") without specifics
- Translate literally — adapt culturally

## CANVA / FIGMA SLIDE SPECS

### Carousel Post Dimensions
- Size: 1080×1080px (square) — works for feed + story cropped
- Safe zone: 80px margin all sides for text
- Slide counter: Bottom right, small dots (current/total)

### Slide 1 (Hook) Layout
```
[Top 20%]  ArthaNote logo (white, small, top-left)
[Middle 60%] Large Tamil headline — 2 lines max, centered
              English translation below in smaller italic
[Bottom 20%] Green gradient fade → "Swipe →" prompt
Background:  Dark green (#065f46) or atmospheric shop photo overlay
```

### Feature Slide Layout
```
[Top 15%]   Section label (e.g., "Sales & Tracking")
[Middle 70%] Icon + Feature name (3 rows)
              Each row: emoji icon | bold feature | light description
[Bottom 15%] Emotional one-liner in Tamil, centered, italic
Background:  White or #d1fae5 light green
```

### CTA Slide Layout
```
[Top 30%]   Full Tamil emotional closing quote, large
[Middle 30%] English translation, smaller
[Bottom 40%] Green button: "📲 Download Free"
             App store badge + QR code (optional)
             "Link in bio" small text
Background:  Rich green gradient (#065f46 → #059669)
```
