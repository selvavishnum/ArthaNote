💡 GST Tips for Your Business
  • 5% GST — Grocery, vegetables, essential foods (கடை / தர்கா)
  • 12% GST — Textiles, processed food, footwear
  • 18% GST — Restaurant, hotel, electronics, most services
  • 28% GST — Luxury goods, tobacco, vehicles
  • When ON, every entry amount will auto-split into base + GST
  • GST applies to sales entries. Expenses are not GST-rated by default
  • File your GST returns separately via the GST portal (gstn.gov.in)
