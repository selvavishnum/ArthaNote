1. Who We Are

ArthaNote ("we", "our", "the app") is a digital ledger product operated by Selva Vishnu M, based in Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu, India. ArthaNote helps small retailers track daily sales, expenses, supplier payments, and generate business reports.

Contact: selvavishnu.m@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address (required for login)
  • Display name (optional, used as your profile name)
  • Google profile photo (only if you sign in with Google)

2.2 Business Data

  • Shop names, business type (e.g. grocery, tea shop)
  • Daily ledger entries: sales, expenses, payments (amounts, dates, descriptions)
  • Supplier names and payment records
  • Staff names and roles (added by you)
  • Finance / Chit Fund member names and payment history
  • GST rate preferences

2.3 Usage & Technical Data

  • Login timestamps
  • App crash logs (browser console only — not sent to us)
  • Device type / browser (not stored — used only for PWA compatibility)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the ledger and reporting features you use
  • To sync your data across devices using Firebase
  • To identify your account and restore data on new devices
  • To verify Pro subscription status

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating ArthaNote for you.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud Platform, servers in Asia-South region). Firebase enforces:

  • Encryption at rest (AES-256)
  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
  • Authentication required to read or write your data

A local cache is stored in your browser's localStorage for offline use. This data stays on your device and is not shared.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Auth Google — user authentication
  • Firestore Google — cloud database
  • Razorpay Razorpay Payments Pvt. Ltd. — payment processing for Pro subscription
  • Google Fonts Google — typography (no personal data shared)

We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics, or any other tracking service.

6. Payments (Pro Plan)

Payments for the Pro plan (₹99/month) are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card or UPI details. Razorpay's privacy policy applies to payment data: razorpay.com/privacy.

After a successful payment, we store only your Razorpay Payment ID and Pro expiry date in your account record to verify subscription status.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data inside the app at any time
  • Export: Use the CSV export feature in Reports to download your data
  • Correction: Edit any entry directly in the app
  • Deletion: Email us to delete your account and all associated data permanently. We will complete deletion within 7 business days.
  • Portability: Export your data as CSV at any time

8. Children

ArthaNote is intended for business use by adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18.

9. Cookies & Local Storage

ArthaNote does not use cookies for tracking. We use browser localStorage only to cache your business data for offline access and to remember your preferences (language, GST settings). This data never leaves your device except when synced to Firebase.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of ArthaNote after a policy update constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or concerns: selvavishnu.m@gmail.com

We aim to respond within 2 business days.
